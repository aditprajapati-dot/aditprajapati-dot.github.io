Selected work

Rebuilding complaint handling at CNC24

Operations
Situation

When I took over, complaints weren't consistently registered, resolution time wasn't tracked, and many solved complaints had never been closed. Every complaint waited for an approval.

What I did

Registered every complaint, cleared the open backlog and built dashboards so the team knew exactly what needed doing. Then I simplified decisions: the team resolves complaints directly, escalates only urgent or high-value ones, and issues credit notes itself.

Result

Faster resolutions, a complaint rate down 2.5% year over year, and customer feedback averaging 8+ out of 10 for a full year.

Closing the loop with suppliers

We also started regular feedback sessions with suppliers. I shared each supplier's quality and delivery performance and complaint rate, showed where most mistakes happened and what good looks like, then listened to their problems and fixed what was on our side.

Invoice automation for a family business

AI & automation
Situation

Every invoice for my family's construction business meant manually editing a Word template.

What I did

Built and deployed a lightweight web app with AI-assisted coding: enter the job details, get a finished PDF.

Result

Routine invoices are now created independently, without needing my help each time.
